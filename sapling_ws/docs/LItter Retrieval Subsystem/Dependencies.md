## Litter Retrieval Subsystem Dependencies

The litter retrieval subsystem depends on packages for the AgileX Piper arm, where these can be found and followed at https://github.com/agilexrobotics/piper_ros/tree/humble
and https://github.com/agilexrobotics/piper_sdk.

Certain CAN commands specified may be superseded by the startup in docs/Litter Retrieval Subsystem/Startup.md, so caution is advised.
